A3 — Serif Split-Level with Pill Groups

Design Approach

Combines the editorial warmth of Option C's serif wordmark with Option A's two-tier structure, but reorganizes the hierarchy: page-level navigation (Rankings, Compare, Benchmarks) lives in the top row as serif text links, while view-mode switching (Table, Map, Scatter, Media) is a segmented pill group in the secondary row.

  • Serif nav links: The primary navigation uses Source Serif 4 instead of Inter, matching the logo and giving the bar a distinctly editorial, scholarly quality. No icons — the serif typography is the visual identity.
  • Wordmark + gold dot: No icon badge at all. The name “HistoryRank.” with a gold period is the logo, clean and typographic like a journal masthead.
  • Pill group in secondary row: Maps, Scatter, and Media are reframed as “view modes” of the Rankings data, grouped in a segmented pill with a “View” label. This makes their role as alternative lenses on the same data much clearer.
  • Breadcrumb right-aligned: A minimal breadcrumb sits at the right of the secondary row for wayfinding context.
  • Count badge: The active view shows the figure count (1,847) in a warm-tinted badge, providing live data context.
