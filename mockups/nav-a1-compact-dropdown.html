A1 — Compact Single-Row + "Visualize" Dropdown

Design Approach

Keeps the ribbon accent and logo from Option A but removes the secondary breadcrumb row entirely, making it a tight single-row bar at just 52px. No icons on top-level links — text only for a cleaner look. Icons appear only inside the dropdown to help differentiate Maps/Scatter/Media.

  • Dropdown: Maps, Scatter, and Media are grouped under "Visualize" with a hover-reveal dropdown that includes brief descriptors (Geographic, Correlations, Film & TV).
  • No icons on primary links: Rankings, Compare, and Benchmarks stand as plain text. Cleaner and more typographic.
  • Rounded-square logo: The HR badge uses a 7px radius square instead of a circle, giving it a more modern app-icon feel while still fitting the warm palette.
  • Single row: No breadcrumb row. The figure count and contextual info can live in the page body instead of the nav.
  • Caveats restored: About, Method, and Caveats are all visible as small meta links rather than hiding Caveats.
