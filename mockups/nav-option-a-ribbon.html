Option A — Two-Tier Ribbon

Design Approach

Key idea: A thin gold accent ribbon at the very top anchors the brand, followed by a two-row header. The primary row has the logo and all navigation flattened out (no dropdown), with icons for visual grouping. A secondary contextual row shows breadcrumb-style wayfinding and a figure count.

Improvements over current: Eliminates the "Explore" dropdown — all links are visible at a glance. The secondary row provides context without cluttering the main nav. The gold accent stripe gives a distinctive brand touch that echoes the warm parchment tones. About/Methodology are demoted to smaller text since they're less-used.
