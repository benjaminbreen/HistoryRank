A2 — Icon-Forward with Tooltip Labels

Design Approach

Primary navigation is icon-only buttons with hover tooltips, dramatically reducing the horizontal footprint. The active page expands to show its label (icon + text), making it clear where you are while keeping inactive items compact.

  • Icons-only: All six nav items are 36×36px icon buttons. Hover reveals a dark tooltip with the page name. Extremely compact.
  • Active expansion: The current page's button widens to show its label alongside the icon, providing clear orientation without cluttering the bar.
  • Dark logo mark: The “HR” badge uses a dark charcoal circle with warm copper lettering — inverted from Option A's light parchment, more modern and higher contrast.
  • Vertical separators: A thin divider separates the data-view icons (Rankings, Maps, Scatter, Media) from the analysis tools (Compare, Benchmarks).
  • Right-aligned meta: About, Method, and Caveats are small text links on the right, clearly secondary.
